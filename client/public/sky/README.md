# Antimatter laboratory sky

`qwantani-night-puresky-4k.webp` is a 4096 × 2048 equirectangular conversion of
Poly Haven's **Qwantani Night (Pure Sky)** tonemapped panorama. The photographic
stars and Milky Way are from the downloaded asset, rather than generated stars.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_night_puresky

- Processing: Jarod Guest

The original 8192 × 4096 JPG was resized with Lanczos sampling and encoded as
WebP quality 90. The scene material applies a cool, dim tint to fit the lab;
the distributed panorama retains its original colors. The 4K image preserves
small stars while keeping the download below 1 MB. No external requests are
needed at runtime. Attribution is optional under CC0 and is retained here for
asset provenance.
